# Herbs + Pork + Potatoes

## Ingredients

- olive oil
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, sliced finely
- ~2 lb pork shoulder, cut into chunks
- salt
- pepper
- celery
- white wine, ~1/2 cup
- 5–6 potatoes, cut into bite size pieces (red/gold)
- ~2 cup chicken broth
- carrots, sliced
- celery, sliced
- 1/2 T dried basil
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary

## Steps

1. Brown the garlic, shallots, and pork, reduce the wine, then simmer the vegetables in broth 45 min–1 hour.

## Full

1. High heat, cook garlic and shallots in cast iron skillet until lightly golden. Toss in lightly salted pork.
2. After pork is browned, add white wine and reduce til 1/2 sauce is left. Then, add potatoes, carrots, and celery, immediately add 1 cup chicken broth.
3. Mix veggies. Mix in herbs. Turn heat down to medium. Simmer and let reduce.
4. Add remaining chicken broth when 1/2 sauce left. Turn heat to low. Simmer for 45 min–1 hour.
