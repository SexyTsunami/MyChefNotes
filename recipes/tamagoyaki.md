# Tamagoyaki

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 Tbsp oil (vegetable), for cooking
- Dashi mixture: 3 Tbsp dashi
- Dashi mixture: 1/2 tsp sugar
- Dashi mixture: 1 tsp soy sauce
- Dashi mixture: 1 tsp mirin
- Dashi mixture: 2 pinches salt
- Or, instead of the dashi mixture: 1 Tbsp ogatsu soup base, or 2 Tbsp water + sugar

## Steps

1. After frying, form the roll with a sushi mat using rubber bands for 5 min.
