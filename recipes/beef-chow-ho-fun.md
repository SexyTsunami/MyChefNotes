# Beef Chow Ho Fun

## Ingredients

- fresh wide rice noodles
- beef, thinly sliced
- grated ginger
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green onions, cut into short pieces
- stock
- 1 Tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 Tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp oyster sauce
- sesame oil
- cooking wine
- pepper
- tapioca flour to thicken
- 1 egg white, beaten

## Steps

1. Cook beef lightly salted. Set aside.
2. Sauté ginger, garlic, green onions, then add meat.
3. Mix sauces, stock, and tapioca flour and egg. Add mixture to meat and veggies. Add the noodles. Stir gently until combined.
