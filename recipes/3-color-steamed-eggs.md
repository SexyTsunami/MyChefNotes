# 3 Color Steamed Eggs

## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- chicken broth, 1:1 with eggs volume
- preserved eggs, sliced
- salted eggs (no egg whites), yolks
- water (silkier)
- ground pepper
- fish sauce
- green onions

## Steps

1. Line plate with aluminum foil. Very low simmer to avoid bubbles. Steam for 40 min until firm.
