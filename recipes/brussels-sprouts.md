# Brussels Sprouts

## Ingredients

- 1–2 Tbsp coconut oil
- bag of brussels sprouts, cut in half
- 2 Tbsp butter
- salt
- pepper

## Steps

1. Sear cut-side down, roast at 400°F about 20 min, then toss with butter, salt, and pepper.

## Full

1. Heat oven 400°. Put coconut oil on cast iron skillet. Place sprouts face down. Sizzle on stove top, ~1 min.
2. Put skillet in oven for 8–10 min.
3. Remove from oven. Shake it up.
4. Put skillet in oven for 10–12 min.
5. Remove from oven and put butter on sprouts and salt and pepper to taste.
6. Sauté for 1–2 min.
