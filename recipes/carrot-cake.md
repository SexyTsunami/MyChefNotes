# Carrot Cake

## Details

8 inch round x 1

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp cloves
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 lb carrots (~3 large carrots), grated
- less than 3/4 cups sugar
- 1/ cup brown sugar
- 2 large eggs (beat 5 min)
- 3/4 cup oil
- 3/4 cup walnuts

## Steps

1. Mix the dry ingredients with the carrots and the wet ingredients, then bake at 325°F for 40–45 min.

## Full

1. Whisk dry ingredients. Mix carrots into flour.
2. Beat eggs. Add sugar + oils.
3. Whisk dry and wet ingredients, then add nuts.
Bake 325° for 40–45 min.
