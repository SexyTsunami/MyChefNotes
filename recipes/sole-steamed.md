# Sole Steamed

## Ingredients

- cornstarch to coat fish
- fillet of flounder or sole
- ginger, sliced thinly
- soy sauce + cornstarch
- sugar
- oyster sauce
- water
- cilantro
- thinly sliced green onions
- hot oil

## Steps

1. Steam 5 min, then drain water. Add sauce. Add ginger. Steam ~5–10 min. Put fresh cilantro and green onions.
