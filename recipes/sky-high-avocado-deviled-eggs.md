# Sky-High Avocado Deviled Eggs

## Details

Deviled eggs (or even halved jammy eggs) are an easy, elegant snack that can be as embellished or casual as needed. Here, the mayo is swapped with avocado for a green filling and topped with Pecan Dukkah, so they are like bites of avocado toast without the toast. Leftovers can go into the curry egg tea sandwiches.

Make it fancy: Sprinkle over other favorite avo-toast toppings like chili crisp or minced chives.

## Ingredients

- 8 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/4 Cup mashed avocado
- 1 Tbs lemon or lime juice
- Salt and black pepper

## Optional

- Pecan Dukkah
- Chili crisp or minced chives

## Steps

1. Blend the filling smooth, then pipe it into the whites.

## Full

Make the filling: Into the bowl of a mini food processor fitted with a steel blade, place 2 whole eggs (sacrifice the ugly eggs to the food processor), the avocado, lemon juice, and a pinch each of salt and pepper and set aside.

Using a sharp knife, cut the remaining eggs in half across the center lengthwise. Add the yolks to the processor bowl with the other ingredients, and arrange the emptied egg whites on the platter. (Using your knife, trim a small piece off the bottom of the egg white halves so they sit up straight.) Blend the ingredients on high until everything is completely smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides of the processor bowl as needed. Taste and season with more lemon juice, salt, and pepper as desired.

Stuff the eggs: Fill the eggs carefully using a couple small spoons or transfer the filling to a zip-top or pastry bag fitted with a star tip, and pipe the mixture into the center of the egg whites. Sprinkle with the Pecan Dukkah (if using). Serve, or cover loosely, and chill for up to 4 days, until ready to serve.
