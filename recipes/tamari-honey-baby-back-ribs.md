# Tamari-Honey Baby Back Ribs

## Details

A crossed-out line before step 2 said "Cover ribs in sauce. Place in."

## Ingredients

- 1 whole rack ribs
- 1/2 cup honey (avocado honey)
- 1 cup tamari
- 4 cloves garlic
- cilantro

## Steps

1. Simmer the honey-tamari sauce 5 min. Steam the ribs at 325°F for 30–45 min, then baste and bake uncovered about 30 min.

## Full

1. Bring to boil (honey, tamari and garlic). Simmer 5 min.
2. 325° oven. Place ribs in sheet. Put water to cover baking pan to steam. Cover with aluminum foil for 30–45 min.
3. Remove from oven and drain water. Pour sauce on ribs and bake uncovered. Baste frequently. Cook for 30 min or until cooked thoroughly.
4. Garnish with cilantro and serve immediately.
