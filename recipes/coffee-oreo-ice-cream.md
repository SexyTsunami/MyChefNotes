# Coffee Oreo Ice Cream

## Ingredients

- 2 cups half and half
- 4 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 cup coffee beans
- 1/2 cup Oreo cookies, broken
- 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
- pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Heat to no more than 170°F. Temper egg yolks. Cook until thick and creamy.
