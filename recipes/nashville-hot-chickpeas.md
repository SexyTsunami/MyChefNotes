# Nashville Hot Chickpeas

## Ingredients

- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- 1/2 tsp chipotle chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp creole seasoning
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 Tbs butter
- 1 Tbs honey

## Steps

1. Toss with the spices. Toast in butter over medium heat 10–12 minutes, then coat with honey.

## Full

Dry the chickpeas: Pat the chickpeas dry with a couple sheets of paper towel, or give them a spin in the salad spinner.

Season the chickpeas: Add the chickpeas to a bowl with the chipotle chili powder, smoked paprika, creole seasoning, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, and salt, and toss to coat.

Toast the chickpeas: In a skillet over medium heat, melt the butter and add the chickpeas. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes, tossing frequently, until toasted. Remove from the heat, add the honey, and toss a final time to coat. Allow to cool.

## Ingredients

- chickpeas, 1 (15-ounce) can, drained and rinsed
- chipotle chili powder, 1/2 teaspoon
- smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon
- creole seasoning, 1/2 teaspoon
- cayenne pepper, 1/2 teaspoon

- kosher salt, 1/4 teaspoon
- unsalted butter, 2 tablespoons
- honey, 1 tablespoon

## Instructions

**Dry the chickpeas:** Pat the chickpeas dry with a couple sheets of paper towel, or give them a spin in the salad spinner.

**Season the chickpeas:** Add the chickpeas to a bowl with the chipotle chili powder, smoked paprika, creole seasoning, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, and salt, and toss to coat.

**Toast the chickpeas:** In a skillet over medium heat, melt the butter and add the chickpeas. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes, tossing frequently, until toasted. Remove from the heat, add the honey, and toss a final time to coat. Allow to cool.
