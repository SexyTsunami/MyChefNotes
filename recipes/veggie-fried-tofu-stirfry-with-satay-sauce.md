# Veggie Fried Tofu Stirfry with Satay Sauce

## Ingredients

- garlic, minced
- green onions, chopped
- veggie oil
- red bell peppers, chopped into chunks
- cabbage, chopped
- mushrooms
- celery
- fried tofu, sliced
- 2 Tbsp satay sauce
- 1 tsp red chili flakes

- chicken stock
