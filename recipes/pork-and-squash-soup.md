# Pork and Squash Soup

## Ingredients

- water
- 2 lb pork neck bones
- 1 squash (zucchini, wintermelon), peeled, cubed
- fish sauce
- salt
- green onions
- ground black pepper

## Steps

1. Boil neck bones for 40 min in water.
2. Add squash and green onions and flavoring.
3. Boil until squash is well-cooked.
