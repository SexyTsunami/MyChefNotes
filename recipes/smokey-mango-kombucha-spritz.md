# Smokey Mango Kombucha Spritz

## Details

Kombucha settles my stomach after dinner. This mimics mezcal margaritas from early Dallas patio nights, and it is gentler. It only calls for things that are already on a standard grocery haul.

Make it fancy: Before filling the glasses, roll the edges in more paprika and/or salt and top with a dehydrated wheel of citrus.

## Ingredients

- 1 heaping Cup ripe mango
- 1/2 Cup orange juice (2 oranges)
- 1/4 Cup lime juice (2 limes)
- 2 Tbs honey or agave
- 1 Cup water
- Ice
- 1 (16 oz) bottle kombucha or citrus seltzer
- Smoked paprika
- A pinch of salt

## Optional

- Paprika and salt for the rims
- Dehydrated citrus wheel

## Steps

1. Blend the mango mix. Pour 4 ounces over ice and top with kombucha.

## Full

Puree the fruit: In a high-powered blender, blend the mango chunks, orange juice, lime juice, honey, and 1 cup water on high until smooth. Transfer the puree to a lidded container and chill until ready to use or use immediately.

To make the drink: Fill four glasses with ice (preferably large format, so it melts slowly) and pour 4 ounces of the puree over top of each glass. Top with kombucha and sprinkle with paprika and salt and serve.

Tip: Garnish the rims of your cocktail: Brush the rim or side of the glass with water, juice, honey, or something else edible that will help dry debris stick. Sprinkle over things like citrus zest; flavored or coarse salt; spices; crushed, freeze-dried fruit; flavored sugars; or anything else finely ground and fitting for the drink, even ingredients like fine cookie crumbs or espresso powder.

Kombucha settles my stomach after dinner. I make this to mimic the thrill of the Dallas patio date nights I shared early in our romance. With our bistro table stacked with emptied chip baskets and little saucers of salsa, we sipped mezcal margaritas and talked for hours. I can't separate those drinks from the happy glimmers of my early twenties. Thankfully, these are much gentler on my body and come without the side of a migraine, which I consider a generous act of self-love. Doubly so because this recipe only calls for things that are already on my standard grocery store haul.

## Ingredients

- ripe mango chunks, 1 heaping cup, fresh (from 1 large) or frozen and thawed
- navel oranges, 2, juiced (about 1/2 cup)
- limes, 2, juiced (about 1/4 cup)
- honey or agave, 2 tablespoons
- ice cubes (preferably 2 x 2-inch large-format ice cubes if you have them)
- kombucha (flavor of the day) or citrusy seltzer water, 1 (16-ounce) bottle
- smoked paprika, for sprinkling
- kosher salt, a pinch

## Make It Fancy

Before filling the glasses, roll the edges in more paprika and/or salt (see Tip) and top with a dehydrated wheel of citrus.

## Instructions

**Puree the fruit:** In a high-powered blender, blend the mango chunks, orange juice, lime juice, honey, and 1 cup water on high until smooth. Transfer the puree to a lidded container and chill until ready to use or use immediately.

**To make the drink:** Fill four glasses with ice (preferably large format, so it melts slowly) and pour 4 ounces of the puree over top of each glass. Top with kombucha and sprinkle with paprika and salt and serve.

## Tip

Garnish the rims of your cocktail: Brush the rim or side of the glass with water, juice, honey, or something else edible that will help dry "debris" stick. Sprinkle over things like citrus zest; flavored or coarse salt; spices; crushed, freeze-dried fruit; flavored sugars; or anything else finely ground and fitting for the drink, even ingredients like fine cookie crumbs or espresso powder—think chocolate martinis!
