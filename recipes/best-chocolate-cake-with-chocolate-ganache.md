# Best Chocolate Cake with Chocolate Ganache

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup bittersweet chocolate (~85 g), melted
- 1 cup brewed coffee (3/4 Tbsp instant coffee to 1 cup water)
- 1 1/2 cup sugar = 300 gm
- 1 2/3 cup flour = 213 g
- 1 cup Dutch cocoa = 100 g
- 1 1/2 tsp baking soda = 6 g
- 1/2 tsp baking powder = 2 g
- 1/4–3/4 tsp salt = 3 g
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup veggie oil
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Ganache: 2/3 cup heavy whipping cream
- Ganache: 2 1/3 cup bittersweet chocolate (2/3) and semisweet (1/3) or 50/50 for half and half sweeter ratio
- Ganache: 1/4 cup butter
- Ganache: 1 Tbsp corn syrup

## Steps

1. Beat the eggs and wet ingredients, fold in the sifted dry mix, and bake at 320°F for 40–45 min. Microwave the ganache and pour it over the cooled cakes.

## Full

1. Preheat oven to 320°F.
2. Beat eggs for 5 min. Add all wet ingredients.
3. Sift dry ingredients. Add wet ingredients.
4. Pour batter in two lined 8" round tins. Parchment each.
5. Bake for 40–45 min, until toothpick clean.
6. Let cool completely.
1. Make sure to cut cakes until even.
2. Microwave ingredients in 30 sec intervals until melted to desired consistency.
3. If too thin, let cool and pour ganache slowly onto cake.
