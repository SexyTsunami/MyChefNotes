# Fish Congee

## Ingredients

- 1–1.5 lb tilapia / cod
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp rice wine
- thinly sliced ginger
- sea salt to taste
- 1–1.5 cups rice
- water
- cilantro and green onions to garnish
- ground white pepper

## Steps

1. Make congee (1.5–2 hrs).
2. Slice fish into diagonal bite-sized pieces. Marinate with ingredients above.
