# Hippy Hemp Dressing

## Details

Hemp hearts are full of protein, and they make a creamy salad dressing without eggs or dairy. Blended into a paste, they are milder than tahini. Use this as an everyday house salad dressing.

Make it fancy: Turn it into a broccoli Waldorf. Toss 2 cups chopped broccoli; 1 cup halved seedless red grapes; 1/2 cup sliced celery; 1 cup chopped apples; 1/4 cup toasted nuts or seeds; 1/4 cup dried cranberries or cherries; and 1/2 cup of this dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 Cup hemp hearts or untoasted sesame seeds
- 1/2 Cup water
- 1/4 Cup avocado oil or olive oil
- 1/4 Cup balsamic vinegar
- 2 small garlic cloves
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp salt

## Optional

- 2 Tbs more avocado oil and 2 Tbs more balsamic, for a thicker dressing

## Steps

1. Blend 1–2 minutes until thick.

## Full

Blend the dressing: In a high-powered blender, combine the hemp hearts, 1/2 cup water, the avocado oil, balsamic vinegar, garlic, maple syrup, and salt and blend on high for 1 to 2 minutes, until completely smooth and thick. The longer you blend it the thicker it will get. Add more water to thin if needed.

Store: Transfer to a lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 4 days.

Tip: Add an extra 2 tablespoons avocado oil and 2 tablespoons balsamic for an extra-thick dressing.

Hemp hearts are full of protein (more than chia seeds and on par with eggs), and they make a luxuriously creamy salad dressing without any added eggs or dairy. I also love that when blended into a paste, hemp hearts are much milder in flavor than tahini, my other favorite dressing base. Use this as your everyday house salad dressing when you get tired of the same ol' lemon and oil lettuce rub down.

## Ingredients

- hemp hearts, or sub untoasted sesame seeds in a pinch, 1/2 cup
- avocado oil or olive oil, 1/4 cup
- balsamic vinegar, 1/4 cup
- garlic cloves, 2 small
- pure maple syrup, 1 teaspoon
- kosher salt, 1 teaspoon

## Make It Fancy

**Turn it into a broccoli Waldorf:** In a large bowl toss together: 2 cups chopped broccoli florets; 1 cup seedless red grapes, halved; 1/2 cup sliced celery; 1 cup chopped apples; 1/4 cup of some toasted nut or seed (walnuts, pecans, sliced almonds, sunflower seeds, pine nuts); 1/4 cup dried cranberries or dried cherries; and 1/2 cup of this Hippy Hemp Dressing and serve.

## Instructions

**Blend the dressing:** In a high-powered blender, combine the hemp hearts, 1/2 cup water, the avocado oil, garlic, maple syrup, and salt and blend on high for 1 to 2 minutes, until completely smooth and thick. The longer you blend it the thicker it will get. Add more water to thin if needed.

Balsamic vinegar is listed with the ingredients. The blend step on the page does not name it. The tip calls for extra balsamic.

**Store:** Transfer to a lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 4 days.

## Tip

Add an extra 2 tablespoons avocado oil and 2 tablespoons balsamic for an extra-thick dressing.
