# Stewed Pork and Eggs

## Ingredients

- 4–5 lb pork shoulder, cubed
- fish sauce
- dark soy sauce
- light soy sauce (a little)
- sugar, if just using unsweetened coconut juice
- hard boiled eggs, rolled around in dark soy sauce
- 1 can Coco Rico

## Steps

1. Marinate the pork over 1 hour, simmer it in Coco Rico about 1.5 hours, then add the eggs for 20–30 min.

## Full

1. Marinate pork for more than 1 hour.
2. Hard boil eggs.
3. In Dutch oven, cook pork on med-high until halfway cooked, then add Coco Rico. When almost fully cooked, turn down heat and simmer for 1 1/2 hrs.
4. Add eggs and simmer for another 20–30 min.
5. Serve with rice or wrap.
For wrap, mix stew sauce with made-up fish sauce.
Wrap ingredients: lettuce, banh trang, rice noodles, bean sprouts, cucumbers, rau ram.
