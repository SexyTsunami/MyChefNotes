# Hot Fudge Sauce (no corn syrup)

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup organic brown sugar
- 1/2 cup agave nectar
- 2/3 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 6 ounces bittersweet chocolate
- 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Steps

1. Stir cocoa powder, sugar, nectar, cream and salt. Add chocolate. Low boil. Stir occasionally for 5 min.
2. Remove pan from heat. Add butter and vanilla. Stir until smooth.
