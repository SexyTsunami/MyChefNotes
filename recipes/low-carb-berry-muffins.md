# Low Carb Berry Muffins

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cup almond flour
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup coconut oil, measured solid, then melted
- 1/3 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 3 eggs, room temp
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup blueberries

## Optional

- lemon zest
- 2 tsp lemon juice

## Steps

1. Stir the dry ingredients with the oil, milk, eggs, and vanilla, fold in the berries, and bake at 350°F for 20 min.

## Full

1. Preheat oven to 350°.
2. In a large bowl, stir the dry ingredients.
3. Mix in melted coconut oil, almond milk, eggs, and vanilla.
4. Fold in berries.
5. Bake 20 min.
