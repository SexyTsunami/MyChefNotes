# Blueberry Lemon Sauce

## Details

Can be made 4 days ahead.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 4 tsp cornstarch
- 6 Tbsp sugar
- 4 tsp lemon juice
- 2 1/2 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen
- lemon zest

## Steps

1. Dissolve cornstarch in water in a saucepan.
2. Stir in 1/2 of the blueberries. Bring to a boil and reduce heat, crushing berries.
3. Stir in the remaining berries. Add lemon.
