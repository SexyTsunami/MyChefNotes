# Pico de Gallo

## Ingredients

- 6 tomatoes (not too ripe)
- 1 onion (medium-sized)
- 1/2 bunch cilantro
- 1 lime juice (fresh)
- 1/2 jalapeño pepper
- 1/4 tsp salt
- pepper

## Steps

1. Dice, mix with lime, season, and rest at least 4 hours.

## Full

1. Dice tomatoes and place in colander to drain.
2. Dice other veggies and mix together with lime juice.
3. Add salt and pepper to taste.
4. Let rest for at least 4 hours in fridge so flavors can marry.
