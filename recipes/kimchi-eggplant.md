# Kimchi Eggplant

## Ingredients

- eggplant
- sesame oil
- soy sauce
- salt to taste
- sugar
- dried red chili pepper flakes
- minced garlic

## Optional

- green onions

## Steps

1. Steam eggplant until soft, but still has its shape.
2. Cool eggplant.
3. Mix rest of ingredients. Serve cold.
