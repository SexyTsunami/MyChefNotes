# Sesame Ginger Eggplant

## Ingredients

- olive oil
- 1 Chinese eggplant, bite sized pieces
- chicken broth, 1/2 cup
- 1 Tbsp light brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp chile
- Sauce: 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- Sauce: 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- Sauce: 1/2 tsp ginger powder / fresh ginger, finely chopped
- Sauce: 1 Tbsp cornstarch / tapioca starch
- Sauce: 2 green onions, chopped
- Sauce: sesame seeds, toasted
- Sauce: garlic
- Sauce: fresh cilantro sprigs for garnish

## Steps

1. Cook the eggplant, then stir in the sesame-soy mix and simmer until thick.
