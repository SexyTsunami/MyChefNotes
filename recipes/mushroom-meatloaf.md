# Mushroom Meatloaf

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 minced onion
- less than 1 tsp salt
- less than 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 cup dried Italian bread, soaked and wrung dry (in water)
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- less than 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp sriracha sauce
- 1/4 tsp thyme
- 2 cups of sliced mushrooms
- Topping: 1/2 sliced onion
- Topping: 1 cup of sliced mushrooms
- Topping: 2 Tbsp salted butter
- Topping: 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Topping: ground pepper

## Steps

1. Mix and bake at 350°F about 1.5 hours to 160°F, then sauté the onion and mushrooms for the sauce.

## Full

1. Mix ingredients. Bake 350° for ~1.5 hours until internal temp is 160°. Place 2 slices of bread on the bottom to soak up grease.
2. Sauté the starred ingredients.
3. Serve with ketchup.
