# Chicken Yakisoba

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 6 pieces chicken thigh, cubed 1"
- 2 tsp–3 tsp chili sauce
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp brown sugar
- 2/3 cup soy sauce (half for chicken, half for noodles)
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1/3 cabbage, sliced
- 1 carrot, sliced thinly
- 1 small crown broccoli
- 1/2 big block of yakisoba noodles
- < 1/3 cup chicken stock

## Steps

1. Cook the chicken with chili sauce, brown sugar, and soy, then the vegetables, then the noodles with soy and broth.

## Full

1. In cast iron skillet, heat oils and add garlic. Add chili sauce. Add chicken.
2. When chicken is halfway cooked, add 2 tsp brown sugar. Add 1/3 cup soy sauce.
3. Remove chicken from skillet when fully cooked.
4. Start cooking vegetables. Remove from skillet when cooked.
5. Add more oil to skillet. Cook noodles. Add 1/3 cup soy sauce. Add some broth. Cook until warm. Add chicken and vegetables. Serve.
