# Pork Laab (Larb)

## Ingredients

- 1/3–2/3 lime juice
- 1 lb pork, ground
- <2–3 Tbsp fish sauce
- 1/2 Tbsp chili flakes
- 1 thinly sliced shallot
- 2 sliced green onions
- bunch of basil, Thai
- 1/4 cup roasted peanuts

## Optional

- cucumber
- lettuce

## Steps

1. Marinate the pork in lime, cook it, then toss with basil and peanuts off the heat. Serve on lettuce.

## Full

1. Marinate pork in lime juice for a few minutes.
2. Cook pork in skillet. Add rest of ingredients except for basil and peanuts.
3. Add basil and peanuts to skillet when heat off.
4. Serve on top of lettuce with slices of cucumber and tomatoes.
