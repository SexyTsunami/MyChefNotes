# Key Lime Pie (no cook filling)

## Ingredients

- Crust: 8 whole graham crackers, broken
- Crust: 2 Tbsp sugar
- Crust: 5 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- Filling: 1/4 cup sugar
- Filling: 1 Tbsp grated lime zest
- Filling: 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- Filling: 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- Filling: 1/3 cup instant vanilla pudding mix
- Filling: 1 1/4 tsp unflavored gelatin
- Filling: 1 cup fresh lime juice (~6–8 limes)
- Filling: 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Bake the crust at 350°F for 12–14 min. Blend the filling with the lime gelatin, pour it in, and chill at least 3 hours.

## Full

1. For crust: oven rack in middle, pulse crackers and sugar in food processor until fine. Add melted butter. Use measuring cup to press firmly into 9" pie plate. Bake at 350° for 12 to 14 min. Cool completely.
2. For filling: process sugar and zest until bright green, ~30 sec. Add cream cheese. Add condensed milk and pudding mix and process until smooth. Stir gelatin with 2 Tbsp lime juice in small bowl and microwave for 15 sec. Stir until dissolved. Pour gelatin mixture in while machine is running. Add remaining juice and vanilla. Mix until combined.
3. Pour filling into crust. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours or up to 2 days. To serve, let pie sit at room temp for 10 min before slicing.
