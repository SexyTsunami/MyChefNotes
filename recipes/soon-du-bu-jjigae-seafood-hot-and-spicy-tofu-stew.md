# Soon Du Bu Jjigae (Seafood Hot and Spicy Tofu Stew)

## Ingredients

- Group A, boiled for the broth: 12 dried anchovies
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1/3 c dried kelp
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 5 cloves garlic
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 4 shiitake mushrooms
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1/2 onion
- Group A, boiled for the broth: beef for stewing
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 4 green onions
- Group A, boiled for the broth: olive oil
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1 green chili pepper
- Group A, boiled for the broth: sesame oil
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 2 Tbsp hot pepper flakes
- Group A, boiled for the broth: shrimp
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1 cup mixed seafood
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1 box soft tofu
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 2 Tbsp fish sauce
- Group A, boiled for the broth: 1 egg per serving

## Steps

1. Boil the broth 30 min, brown the beef, then simmer the seafood, tofu, and eggs 10–15 min.

## Full

1. Make fish broth by boiling ingredients A for 30 minutes in 5 cups of water.
2. Take out mushrooms and slice. Heat claypot with oil and brown beef. Add green onions. Add pepper flakes. Add 2 cups stock. Add seafood. Add fish sauce. Add tofu and eggs.
3. Cook/boil for another 10–15 min and serve.
