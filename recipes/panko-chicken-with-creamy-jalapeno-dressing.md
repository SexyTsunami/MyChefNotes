# Panko Chicken with Creamy Jalapeño Dressing

## Ingredients

- chicken, thin 1/4" pieces, lightly salted
- egg
- panko mixed with salt and pepper
- lettuce / cabbage / salad mix / radishes
- 6 cups thinly shredded cabbage
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- 1 cup sliced radishes
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves
- Jalapeño dressing: 1/2 cup mayo
- Jalapeño dressing: 1/4 cup buttermilk
- Jalapeño dressing: 1/4 cup scallions
- Jalapeño dressing: 1 jalapeño, seeded, chopped
- Jalapeño dressing: 2 Tbsp fresh lime juice
- Jalapeño dressing: 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Jalapeño dressing: 1/2 tsp cumin

## Optional

- Jalapeño dressing: 1/2 cup cilantro
