# Kale Quinoa Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 cups water
- 1 bunch kale, bite sized pieces
- 1/2 avocado, diced
- 1/2 cup chopped cucumber
- 1/3 cup chopped red bell pepper
- 2 Tbsp chopped onion (red)
- 1 Tbsp crumbled feta / goat cheese
- Dressing: 1/4 cup olive oil
- Dressing: 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- Dressing: 1 1/2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
- Dressing: 3/4 tsp sea salt
- Dressing: 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook quinoa. Microwave 4 min, stir, microwave 2 min, let sit.
2. Steam kale to remove bitter flavor.
3. Mix ingredients. Pour dressing over. Sprinkle with cheese. Serve.
