# Tart Shell

## Details

One 9" tart pan

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 8 Tbsp butter, unsalted, 1/2" pieces
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 Tbsp heavy cream

## Steps

1. Mix the dough, chill at least 1 hour, then bake at 375°F for 25–30 min with weights and 5 min more until golden.

## Full

1. Whisk yolks, cream, and vanilla in a bowl. In food processor, process flour, sugar and salt. Add butter cubes and process into a cornmeal texture. Add wet ingredients.
2. Wrap dough and shape into disc. Refrigerate for at least one hour.
3. Remove from fridge and let sit for 10–15 min before rolling out between plastic wrap / parchment paper. Remove one side and place in tart pan. Put in fridge/freezer for 5 min. Use fork to poke holes in tart.
4. Bake at 375°F, middle rack, with pie weights for 25–30 min. Remove and bake for additional 5 min until tart is golden.
