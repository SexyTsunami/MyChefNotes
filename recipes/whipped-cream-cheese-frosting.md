# Whipped Cream Cheese Frosting

## Ingredients

- 8 oz cream cheese, room temperature
- 3/4 Cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 cup heavy whipped cream

## Steps

1. Whip cream in separate bowl.
2. Whip cream cheese and sugar and vanilla in another bowl until fluffy.
3. Combine both mixtures. Do not overbeat.
