# Hummus

## Ingredients

- 1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (~1 lemon)
- 1/4 cup tahini
- 1 large garlic clove
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for serving
- 1/2–1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin, ground
- 2–3 Tbsp water
- 3 Tbsp sour cream
- dash of paprika
