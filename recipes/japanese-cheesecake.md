# Japanese Cheesecake

## Ingredients

- 130 ml milk
- 100 g cream cheese
- 100 g butter
- 8 egg yolks
- 60 g flour
- 60 g cornstarch
- 13 egg whites
- 130 g granulated sugar
- strawberries, powdered sugar to serve

## Steps

1. Cook the cream cheese base, fold in stiff egg whites, and bake in a water bath at 320°F for 25 min, then 280°F for 55 min.

## Full

1. Preheat oven to 320°F. Heat milk, cream cheese, and butter. Whisk until smooth.
2. In a large bowl, whisk yolks until smooth, then slowly drizzle in the cream mixture, stirring until combined. Sift in flour and cornstarch. Make sure no lumps.
3. In another large bowl, beat egg whites with a hand mixer until soft peaks. Gradually add sugar and beat until stiff peaks form.
4. Add egg whites to the mixture 1/4 at a time.
5. Pour batter into pan (lined with parchment, 4-inch strip and bottom). Shake to release bubbles. Place in a larger baking dish with hot water, 1 inch water.
6. Bake at 320°F for 25 min, then reduce heat to 280°F for 55 min. Bake until risen double. Invert cake onto a plate and remove paper.
