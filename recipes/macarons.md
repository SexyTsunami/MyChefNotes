# Macarons

## Details

Variations
Measurements for 8–10 macarons.
Black sesame
Remove 1 Tbsp almond flour and add 1 Tbsp ground black sesame. Food process black sesame with powdered sugar before sifting with almond flour.
Raspberry almond
Macerate 8–10 raspberries in sugar. Cut in half for each macaron.
Buttercream:
- 3 Tbsp butter
- 1 Tbsp heavy cream
- 1/4 tsp almond extract
Pipe circle of buttercream and place raspberry on top.
Mexican chocolate
Remove 5 g almond flour and add 5 g of Dutch cocoa. Add pinch of cinnamon.
Make cinnamon + dark chocolate ganache (semisweet and bittersweet choc chips).
Salted caramel
Toast 1/3 of almond meal.
Filling for 20 macarons:
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 1/2 Tbsp water
- 1/2 cup sugar + 1/8 cup corn syrup
- 1/4 tsp salt
1. Melt butter and cream in glass measuring cup, and salt too.
2. In small pot, heat up water and corn syrup and sugar. Medium heat.
3. When it starts boiling, cover pot for 1 minute.
4. When golden, then almost amber, then add cream mixture. Stir bottom a couple times.
5. When mixture reaches 240° (245° for chewier), pour into heat proof container. Shallow is good. Do not scrape leftover in same container. It will be different texture.
6. Cool for 1.5 hours, then put in piping bag for piping.

## Ingredients

- 60 g powdered sugar
- 45 g almond meal
- 1 egg white (~34 g)
- 25 g granulated sugar
- pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Heat the whites and sugar to 160°F, beat to stiff peaks, fold in the almond mix, dry 1 hour, and bake 12–14 min at 320–325°F.

## Full

1. Make meringue: in double boiler, mix egg whites and granulated sugar and heat to 160°, whisking constantly. Once heated to 160°, remove from heat and beat into stiff peaks.
2. Sift dry ingredients together.
3. Add dry ingredients to meringue, 1/2 at a time. Batter should be like molten lava and level out in 20 sec. Fold in one direction.
4. Pipe onto Silpat or parchment paper. Rap baking sheet to remove air bubbles.
5. Dry for at least one hour.
6. Bake for 12–14 min at 320–325°.
