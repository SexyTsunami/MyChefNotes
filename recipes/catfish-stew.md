# Catfish Stew

## Ingredients

- green onion (white part)
- garlic
- fresh catfish
- dark soy sauce (thick)
- nuoc mam
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- water
- chili peppers

## Steps

1. Start the onion and garlic in oil, lay the fish in one layer, and stew 20 min, turning halfway.

## Full

Use ceramic stew pot.
1. Start onion and garlic in oil.
2. Lay catfish in one layer.
3. Drizzle sauces and sprinkle sugar.
4. Stew for 20 min. Turn fish halfway cooked.
