# Catfish Stewed / Simmered

## Ingredients

- 2–3 lb catfish nuggets
- vegetable oil
- 2–3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- fish sauce
- dark soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp sugar (light brown or white)
- scallions

## Steps

1. In medium-high heat, heat oil in pot and add garlic until almost golden. Remove garlic and set aside.
2. Mix fish sauce, dark soy sauce and sugar.
3. Place fish in pot and drizzle sauce over fish.
