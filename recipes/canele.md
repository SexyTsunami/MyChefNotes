# Canele

## Details

Heat over stove.
Molds at room temp.
Pour mixture back in heating cup (measuring cup).
Set batter out at room temp for 1 hr before filling molds.

## Ingredients

- 1 vanilla bean
- 500 ml milk
- 220 grams sugar
- 100 grams all-purpose flour
- 50 g melted butter
- 2 eggs
- 60 ml rum
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- Mold coating: 30 g beeswax
- Mold coating: 60 g butter

## Steps

1. Simmer the milk, mix the batter, and chill at least 48 hours. Bake at 470°F for 16 min, then 375°F for 56 min.

## Full

1. Place milk, butter, and vanilla pod in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer.
2. Place sugar and eggs in a mixing bowl and beat. Add butter. Add flour and salt and mix until smooth.
3. Add 1/4 of the hot milk to temper the mixture. Slowly mix in the remaining milk. Add rum.
4. Refrigerate for at least 48 hours.
Preheat oven to 470°F. Oven rack in the lower 1/3.
1. Generously grease molds with melted butter + beeswax. (Fill molds with 60–65 g of batter. ~14–15 molds.)
2. Preheat baking sheet. Place filled canele molds on the tray and bake at 470° for 16 min. Then decrease temp to 375° and bake for 56 min. Remove molds immediately. Cool for 1 hr before serving.
