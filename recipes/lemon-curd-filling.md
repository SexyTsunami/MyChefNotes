# Lemon Curd Filling

## Details

Whipped cream

## Ingredients

- 3/4–1 cup sugar
- 1/3 cup cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 5 large egg yolks
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- zest of one lemon
- 2 Tbsp butter
- Whipped cream: 1/2–3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- Whipped cream: 1 Tbsp powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Fill the cooled tart shell.
2. Whip and spread on top of lemon filling.
3. Add blueberries, raspberries, strawberries, kiwi, etc. and serve chilled.
