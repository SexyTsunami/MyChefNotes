# Braised Lamb Shank

## Ingredients

- 3/4 tsp ground coriander seed
- 3/4 tsp ground fennel seed
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/8 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 bone-in lamb shanks (~ 3 lbs)
- salt (kosher) and ground black pepper
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 med. carrots, large dice
- 1 celery stalk, dice
- 1/2 med. onion, dice
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 anchovy fillets
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 2 cups or more chicken stock
- rosemary
- 1 bay leaf

## Steps

1. Brown the shanks, soften the vegetables, then braise at 300°F for 3 hours and blend the sauce.

## Full

1. Preheat oven to 300°. Combine spices and season shanks with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a Dutch oven and brown shanks. Remove shanks.
3. Add veggies to the Dutch oven with oil and cook until softened / browned.
4. Add anchovy fillets and tomato paste and wine.
5. Add shanks. Add stock, rosemary, and bay leaf.
6. Cut parchment paper with a hole in the center to allow for evaporation and place in the pan. (A sketch shows a round of parchment with a small hole in the middle.)
7. Transfer pan to oven and cook for 3 hours.
8. Discard bay leaf and blend veggies and stock with an immersion blender.
9. Serve shanks with braising sauce. Baste the shanks and sauce all over.
