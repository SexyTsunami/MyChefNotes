# Crock Pot Chicken Cacciatore (tomato-based)

## Ingredients

- 1 chicken, skin removed
- 1 can stewed tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 stalks celery
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp sweet basil
- 1/2 tsp rosemary
- 1/2 tsp cilantro
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients and pour on top of chicken. Slow cook on low 6–8 hours.
