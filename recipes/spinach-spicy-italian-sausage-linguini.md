# Spinach Spicy Italian Sausage Linguini

## Ingredients

- 1 package linguini
- 1/2 lb spicy Italian sausage
- 1 bunch of finely chopped basil leaves
- garlic, minced
- salt (may not need)
- pepper
- 1/2 cup Parmesan / pecorino
- 1 large bunch fresh spinach leaves
- 1 onion, finely chopped
