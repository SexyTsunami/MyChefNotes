# Horchata

## Ingredients

- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 4 cups hot water + 2–3 cups water
- 1/2 Tbsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp / 2 sticks cinnamon
- 1 cup white sugar
- 5 cups milk / rice milk
- 2 Tbsp cream if using skim milk

## Steps

1. Soak the rice 4–6 hours or overnight, blend it with cinnamon and vanilla, then mix with sugar and milk and serve cold.

## Full

- Soak 2 cups uncooked rice in 4 cups hot water for at least 4–6 hours. Overnight is best. If using cinnamon sticks/bark, soak with rice.
- Reserved rice water and place soaked rice in food processor and blend into fine granules. Add ground cinnamon and vanilla and blend.
- Place blended mixture into container and mix sugar + milk.
- Serve chilled.
