# Banh Tam Bi

## Ingredients

- thick bun noodles (XL)
- shiso / purple perilla
- rau ram
- mint
- bì
- nuoc mam
- cucumber, sliced thin
- coconut milk (~2 Tbsp per bowl)
- sugar
- soy sauce
- roasted rice flour + fresh garlic
- Bì: country spare rib, cooked in skillet with garlic, add water, cover, slowly braise
- Bì: or buy package, flash boil, rinse in cold water, drain, completely squeeze

## Steps

1. Top the thick noodles with herbs, cucumber, coconut milk, and nuoc mam.
