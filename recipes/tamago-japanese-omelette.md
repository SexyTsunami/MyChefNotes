# Tamago (Japanese Omelette)

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp mirin (rice wine)
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- may put a little dashi powder

## Steps

1. Cook on low-medium heat so the eggs do not brown.

## Full

Cook on low-med heat to avoid browning.
