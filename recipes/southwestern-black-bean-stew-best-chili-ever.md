# Southwestern Black Bean Stew — Best Chili Ever

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 1/2 Tbsp adobo seasoning
- 1 1/2 Tbsp chili seasoning
- 1 can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 can black beans, undrained
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- shredded cheddar cheese
- chopped green onions
- salt to taste

## Steps

1. Brown meat in cast iron skillet. Medium-high.
2. Add seasoning to meat, then add cans and water and sour cream. Medium for 30–45 min.
3. Cheddar cheese and green onions to garnish.
