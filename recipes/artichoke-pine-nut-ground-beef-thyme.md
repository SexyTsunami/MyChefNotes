# Artichoke Pine Nut Ground Beef Thyme

## Ingredients

- 1 clove minced garlic
- 1/2 finely sliced onions
- 1 lb ground beef
- salt
- thyme
- olive oil
- marjoram
- oregano
- parsley
- 1 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup artichoke hearts (Trader Joe's)
- 1 cup fresh basil
- 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts
- shredded pecorino romano
- cook gnocchi or any pasta (tight spiral noodles)
- salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Cook the onion and garlic, brown the thyme beef, then simmer with tomatoes, artichokes, basil, and toasted pine nuts. Serve on pasta.

## Full

Mix the beef, salt, thyme, and olive oil together.
1. Cook onions and garlic until soft and translucent.
2. Mix salt and thyme and olive oil into beef.
3. Add beef mixture into garlic and onions in skillet.
4. Sprinkle marjoram, oregano, and parsley. Add crushed tomatoes. Add artichoke hearts, basil and toasted nuts.
5. Serve on top of pasta and shredded cheese.
