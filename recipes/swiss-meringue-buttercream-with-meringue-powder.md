# Swiss Meringue Buttercream with Meringue Powder

## Details

If storing in fridge, let come to room temp, then beat for several min until smooth.

## Ingredients

- 3 egg whites (6 tsp meringue powder + 6 Tbsp water)
- 12 oz powdered sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 12 oz unsalted butter

## Steps

1. Beat the whites and sugar 5 min, then beat in the butter for 10 min.

## Full

1. Combine egg whites, powdered sugar and salt with paddle on low speed, then medium for 5 min. If coloring frosting, set aside a small amount of meringue. Extremely difficult to color after adding butter.
2. On medium-low, add butter 1–2 Tbsp at a time until well-incorporated. Beat for 10 min. Add coloring if desired.
