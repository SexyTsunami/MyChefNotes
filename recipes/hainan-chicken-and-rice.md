# Hainan Chicken and Rice

## Ingredients

- With the chicken: whole chicken, or just dark meats with bones
- With the chicken: onion (medium)
- With the chicken: green onions
- With the chicken: ginger
- With the chicken: salt
- With the chicken: black pepper
- Rice: add ginger, garlic, chicken fat + broth.
- Sauce: vegetable oil
- Sauce: salt

## Steps

1. Boil the chicken 30–45 min. Soak the rice 15 min, fry the ginger and garlic, then cook the rice in half the broth. Warm the sauce and serve with cucumber and cilantro.

## Full

Serve with cucumber and cilantro.
1. Scrub chicken skin with salt. Season chicken with salt after rinsing clean. Add water to pot and boil the starred ingredients for 30–45 min.
2. Soak rice for 15 min and wash until water clear. Add oil to pot and fry ginger and garlic until fragrant, then add rice. Add 1/2 amount (50%) of broth to rice cooker to cook rice. (Rice is already hydrated from soak.)
3. Make sauce by warming ingredients in saucepan.

## Ingredients

Starred, boiled with the chicken:

- whole chicken, or just dark meats with bones
- onion (medium)
- green onions
- ginger
- salt
- black pepper

Rice: add ginger, garlic, chicken fat + broth.

Sauce, 1:4 garlic to ginger, minced:

- vegetable oil
- salt

Serve with cucumber and cilantro.

## Instructions

1. Scrub chicken skin with salt. Season chicken with salt after rinsing clean. Add water to pot and boil the starred ingredients for 30–45 min.
2. Soak rice for 15 min and wash until water clear. Add oil to pot and fry ginger and garlic until fragrant, then add rice. Add 1/2 amount (50%) of broth to rice cooker to cook rice. (Rice is already hydrated from soak.)
3. Make sauce by warming ingredients in saucepan.
