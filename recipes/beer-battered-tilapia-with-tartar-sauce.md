# Beer-Battered Tilapia with Tartar Sauce

## Ingredients

- ~1.1 lbs of tilapia / cod / whitefish fillets
- Batter: 1/2 cup flour
- Batter: 1/2 bottle beer
- Batter: salt
- Batter: pepper
- Batter: oil
- Tartar sauce: 1/2 c mayo
- Tartar sauce: 1 Tbsp relish
- Tartar sauce: 1 Tbsp minced onion
- Tartar sauce: salt
- Tartar sauce: pepper
- Tartar sauce: 1/2–1 Tbsp lemon
- Tartar sauce: paprika
- Tartar sauce: ~5–6 crushed Ritz crackers, to thicken

## Steps

1. Dip fish in batter.
2. Fry in canola oil for ~5 min, golden brown.
3. Serve immediately with tartar sauce and coleslaw.
