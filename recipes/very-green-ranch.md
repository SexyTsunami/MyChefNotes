# Very Green Ranch

## Details

I have an unrestrained habit of adding herbs to sauces so they turn green—in some way the recipe feels instantly elevated and more nutritious. This herby ranch is actually made with yogurt instead of buttermilk, and it's my favorite thing to put on almost everything you think it should go with—grilled corn, salads, baby carrots, tacos, nachos, simple black beans and rice with a side of fried sweet plantains—and even anywhere else it probably shouldn't, yet my American mind makes it make sense, like French fries or a slice of pizza.

Make it fancy: Roast 1 small jalapeño until tender, and after removing the seeds, blend it along with the other ingredients for an addictive Jalapeño Ranch Dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1 Cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 2 packed Cups cilantro
- 1 handful parsley
- 1/4 Cup fresh dill or 1 tsp dried dill
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 Tbs lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 large scallions

## Optional

- 1 small roasted jalapeño, seeds removed

## Steps

1. Blend until combined.

## Full

Blend the sauce: Into a food processor or high-powered blender, put the yogurt, cilantro, parsley, dill, garlic, lemon juice, paprika, and salt. Tear the scallions into chunks and add them as well. You may need to pack everything in to fit. Blend on high until well combined.

Store: Transfer to a lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 10 days.

## Ingredients

- whole-milk Greek yogurt, 1 cup
- fresh cilantro, 2 packed cups, tender stems are fine, roughly chopped
- fresh flat-leaf parsley, a handful, tender stems are fine, roughly chopped
- fresh dill, 1/4 cup chopped — or 1 teaspoon dried dill or tarragon
- garlic cloves, 4
- lemon juice, 2 tablespoons
- sweet paprika, 1/4 teaspoon
- kosher salt, 1 teaspoon, plus more as needed
- scallions, 2 large, both the white and green parts, roots trimmed

## Make It Fancy

Roast 1 small jalapeño until tender, and after removing the seeds, blend it along with the other ingredients for an addictive Jalapeño Ranch Dressing.

## Instructions

**Blend the sauce:** Into a food processor or high-powered blender, put the yogurt, cilantro, parsley, dill, garlic, lemon juice, paprika, and salt. Tear the scallions into chunks and add them as well. You may need to pack everything in to fit. Blend on high until well combined.

**Store:** Transfer to a lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 10 days.
