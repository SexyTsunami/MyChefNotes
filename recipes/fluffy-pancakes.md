# Fluffy Pancakes

## Details

May freeze up to 2 months, with parchment paper and a freezer bag.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/8 cup granulated sugar
- 4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups milk, or more
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 egg
