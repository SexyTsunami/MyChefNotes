# Eggplant Lasagna with Caramelized Onions

## Ingredients

- 2 small onions, finely sliced
- ~1 lb ground beef (use Italian sausage recipe)
- 1 eggplant, cut into bite sized pieces
- freshly boiled spinach
- olive oil
- 15 oz ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
- 1 egg
- Tomato sauce: 1 jar premade spaghetti sauce
- Tomato sauce: 1 can tomato paste (6 oz)
- Tomato sauce: 2 garlic cloves
- Tomato sauce: 1 tsp sugar
- Tomato sauce: basil
- Tomato sauce: 4–6 oz water
- Tomato sauce: mozzarella cheese, grated
- Tomato sauce: provolone

## Steps

1. Mix the spinach, ricotta, Parmesan, egg, and olive oil for the filling.

## Full

Mix the spinach, olive oil, ricotta, Parmesan, and egg together in a bowl.
