# Pumpkin Cheesecake

## Details

Crust
Cheesecake

## Ingredients

- Crust: 1 1/3 cups almond flour
- Crust: 4 Tbsp butter, melted
- Crust: 1 Tbsp sugar
- Filling: 24 oz cream cheese, softened
- Filling: 3/4 cup sugar
- Filling: 3 eggs, room temp
- Filling: 6 oz canned pumpkin puree
- Filling: 3/4 tsp cinnamon
- Filling: 1/4 tsp ground ginger
- Filling: < 1/8 tsp ground cloves

## Steps

1. Bake the crust at 300°F for 15 min. Beat the filling and bake at 350°F for 50–55 min, then chill overnight.

## Full

1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. Mix almond flour, butter, and sugar. Press into an 8×8 square pan. Bake 15 min.
1. Combine cream cheese and sugar. Beat on low.
2. Add one egg at a time and add spices with pumpkin.
3. Pour into the pan. Bake at 350°F for about 50–55 min.
4. Refrigerate overnight before serving.
