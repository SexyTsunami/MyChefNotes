# Red Potato Salad

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs unpeeled red potatoes
- 4 eggs, hardboiled
- 1 1/2 cups mayo
- 2 Tbsp milk
- 2 Tbsp distilled white vinegar
- 1/2 cup sliced green onions
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 cup sliced celery
- 2 tsp dill relish
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Boil the potatoes about 15 min, mix with the eggs and dressing, and chill about 2 hours.

## Full

1. Bring large pot of salted water to a boil. Add red potatoes and cook until tender but still firm, ~15 minutes. Drain, cool and chop into 1 inch cubes.
2. Peel and dice hard boiled eggs.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together mayo, milk, vinegar, green onions, salt and pepper. Pour mixture into potatoes and mix together with eggs and celery. Cover and chill in refrigerator ~2 hours before serving.
