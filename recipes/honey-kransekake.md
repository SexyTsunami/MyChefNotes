# Honey Kransekake

## Ingredients

- 500 g almond flour
- 300 g icing sugar
- 85 g honey
- 4 egg whites
- 1 tsp almond extract
- Honey icing: 1/4 cup icing sugar + 1 tsp icing sugar
- Honey icing: 1 Tbsp honey
- Honey icing: 1 Tbsp milk
- Honey icing: 3 tsp meringue powder

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients w/ dough hook.
2. Leave overnight in the fridge.
3. Bake at 385° for 8–10 min.
