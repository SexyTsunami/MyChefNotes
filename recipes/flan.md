# Flan

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 can condensed milk
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 tsp vanilla
- pinch of salt
- 4 eggs

## Steps

1. Coat the pan with dark caramel, blend the custard, and bake at 320°F in a water bath about 1 hour. Chill before serving.

## Full

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position. Heat to 320°F.
2. Pour hot water into bain-marie and put 8" round cake pan in water to keep warm.
3. Make dark amber caramel and pour into pan. Swirl to coat pan. Allow to set 5–10 min.
4. Use immersion blender to combine rest of ingredients. Pour into pan.
5. Water should reach halfway up sides of pan when set in water bath.
6. Bake flan until wobbly at center, ~1 hour.
7. Cool for 15 min. Chill in refrigerator 2 to 3 hours or overnight before serving.
