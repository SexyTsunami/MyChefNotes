# Mushroom Risotto

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups arborio rice
- 6 cups chicken broth, divided
- 3 Tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lb portobello mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 lb crimini mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 2 shallots, diced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- pepper and sea salt to taste
- 3 Tbsp chives, finely chopped
- 2–4 Tbsp butter
- 1/3 cup Parmesan / Pecorino Romano cheese, grated

## Steps

1. Soften the mushrooms, toast the rice, stir in wine and then broth until al dente, and finish with butter and cheese.

## Full

1. Warm broth in saucepan on low heat. (Dutch oven.)
2. In large saucepan, 2 tablespoons of olive oil over med-high heat. Stir in mushrooms and cook until soft. Remove from heat. Set aside.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil. Stir shallots (in Dutch oven). Add rice and coat with oil until golden. Add wine, stirring constantly until absorbed. Continue stirring. Add 1/2 cup broth to rice, stirring until liquid is absorbed and rice is al dente.
4. Remove from heat. Add mushrooms and their liquid, butter, chives, and cheese. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
