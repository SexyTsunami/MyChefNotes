# Creamy Italian White Bean Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 (16 oz) cans white kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 (14 oz) can chicken broth
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/8 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley
- 2 cups water/broth
- 1 bunch spinach, thinly sliced
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- grated Parmesan cheese
- A bracket beside the beans through the water/broth says "can use only broth."

## Steps

1. Simmer the onion, celery, beans, and broth 15 min, blend most of it, then stir in spinach and lemon.

## Full

1. In large saucepan, heat oil. Cook onion and celery for 5 to 8 minutes. Add garlic. Stir in beans, chicken broth, pepper, thyme, parsley. Bring to boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 15 minutes.
2. With slotted spoon, remove 2 cups of bean and veggies and set aside.
3. Blend soup at low speed until smooth. Mix blended soup with bean and veggie reserve.
4. Bring to boil, occasionally stirring. Add spinach. Stir in lemon juice. Remove from heat. Serve with grated Parmesan cheese.
