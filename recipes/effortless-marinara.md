# Effortless Marinara

## Details

This is a 15-minute any-night marinara. Tweak it with powdered or freshly minced garlic, a leaf or two of basil or dried oregano, red wine, or whatever you think will bring flavor, and let it simmer longer if you want.

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbs unsalted butter
- 2 Tbs olive oil
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1/4 Cup water
- 1 Tbs onion powder
- 2 tsp salt

## Optional

- Minced garlic, basil, dried oregano, or a splash of red wine

## Steps

1. Cook the paste a few minutes, then simmer 10–15 minutes.

## Full

Cook the tomato paste: In a medium saucepan with a lid over medium heat, melt the butter and olive oil. Once the butter begins to bubble and foam, add the tomato paste and let it cook for a few minutes, whisking until incorporated into the fat.

Make the sauce: Add the crushed tomatoes. Fill the empty can with about 1/4 cup water. Swirl it around to collect any remaining sauce from the sides of the can and add that to the pan as well, along with the onion powder and salt.

Simmer: Bring to a boil then reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer the sauce, partially covered, for 10 to 15 minutes. Taste and season with more salt, if desired.

Tip: Crushed tomatoes are just as delicious as some of the more expensive whole canned tomatoes and easy to doctor up, plus you won't have to crush them yourself.

## Original

**From:** We Fancy

**Makes about 4 cups (32 ounces)**  
**(EF, GF, NF, RSF, S30)**

Because everyone should have their own homemade, simple marinara sauce, here's my 15-minute any-night-of-the-week version. This one is speedy-quick and a solid base recipe that you can tweak with powdered or freshly minced garlic, a leaf or two of basil or dried oregano, red wine, or whatever you think will bring flavor, and let it simmer longer; it's all up to you.

## Ingredients

- unsalted butter, 2 tablespoons
- olive oil, 2 tablespoons
- tomato paste, 1 (6-ounce) can
- crushed tomatoes, 1 (28-ounce) can (see Tip)
- onion powder, 1 tablespoon
- kosher salt, 2 teaspoons, plus more as needed

## Instructions

**Cook the tomato paste:** In a medium saucepan with a lid over medium heat, melt the butter and olive oil. Once the butter begins to bubble and foam, add the tomato paste and let it cook for a few minutes, whisking until incorporated into the fat.

**Make the sauce:** Add the crushed tomatoes. Fill the empty can with about 1/4 cup water. Swirl it around to collect any remaining sauce from the sides of the can and add that to the pan as well, along with the onion powder and salt.

**Simmer:** Bring to a boil then reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer the sauce, partially covered, for 10 to 15 minutes. Taste and season with more salt, if desired.

## Tip

**Cans of pre-crushed tomatoes:** Crushed tomatoes are just as delicious as some of the more expensive whole canned tomatoes out there and easy to doctor up, plus you won't have to dirty your hands crushing them yourself.
