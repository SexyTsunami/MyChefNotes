# Palak Paneer

## Ingredients

- 6 cups uncooked spinach
- 1 cup paneer (milk curdled with vinegar)
- 1 Tbsp oil
- 3/4 cup finely chopped onions
- 3–4 green chilis (Korean works)
- pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2–3/4 cup tomatoes, diced, canned
- salt to taste
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1 Tbsp cream / sour cream / yogurt

## Steps

1. Blanch the spinach, sauté the aromatics, blend, then simmer with cream and paneer.

## Full

1. Blanch spinach. Rinse cool water. Drain.
2. Sauté onions, peppers/chilies, garlic. Add turmeric.
3. Hand-blenderize veggies.
4. Cook in med heat. Add garam masala and salt and cream.
5. Add paneer cut into 1/2" cubes.
