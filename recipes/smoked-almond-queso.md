# Smoked Almond Queso

## Details

This recipe is the reason I keep an obscene stock of smoked nuts in the cabinet. This is a dairy-free queso. Serve as a dip for pretzel sticks, veggies, or tortilla chips.

Make it fancy: Once the queso has been blended, stir in 2 (4-ounce) cans diced green chiles or 1 (10-ounce) can fire roasted diced tomatoes for extra flavor and heat.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 Cup hickory-smoked almonds
- 1 Cup water
- 1/4 Cup lemon juice
- 1 Tbs onion powder
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1/4 Cup nutritional yeast
- 2 tsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Optional

- 2 (4 oz) cans diced green chiles or 1 (10 oz) can fire-roasted tomatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, if the almonds are not smoky

## Steps

1. Blend about 5 minutes until thick and hot.

## Full

Blend the queso: In a high-powered blender, combine the smoked almonds, 1 cup water, lemon juice, onion powder, garlic, nutritional yeast, and tomato paste, and salt, blending on high for about 5 minutes, or until completely smooth and the sauce is ultra thick and hot. Thin with more water, if needed, and taste and season with more salt and more lemon juice as desired.

Store: Transfer to a small lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 1 week.

Tip: If you have almonds that aren't smoky or smoky enough, add a teaspoon or so of smoked paprika to wake them up.

**Makes 2 generous cups**  
**(DF, GF, RSF, S30)**

This recipe is the reason I keep an obscene stock of smoked nuts in the cabinet. It's also evidence of the wonders even just a handful can do. This is a dairy-free queso I make for its simplicity. Serve as a dip for pretzel sticks, veggies, or tortilla chips.

## Ingredients

- hickory-smoked almonds (such as Smokehouse Almonds), 1/2 cup (3 ounces) (see Tip)
- lemon juice, 1/4 cup (from about 2 large lemons), plus more as needed
- onion powder, 1 tablespoon
- garlic cloves, 4
- nutritional yeast, 1/4 cup
- tomato paste, 2 teaspoons
- kosher salt, 1/2 teaspoon, plus more as needed depending on the saltiness of your almonds

## Make It Fancy

Once the queso has been blended, stir in 2 (4-ounce) cans diced green chiles or 1 (10-ounce) can fire roasted diced tomatoes for extra flavor and heat.

## Instructions

**Blend the queso:** In a high-powered blender, combine the smoked almonds, 1 cup water, lemon juice, onion powder, garlic, nutritional yeast, and tomato paste, and salt, blending on high for about 5 minutes, or until completely smooth and the sauce is ultra thick and hot. Thin with more water, if needed, and taste and season with more salt and more lemon juice as desired.

**Store:** Transfer to a small lidded container and keep in the fridge for up to 1 week.

## Tip

If you have almonds that aren't smoky or smoky enough, add a teaspoon or so of smoked paprika to wake them up.
