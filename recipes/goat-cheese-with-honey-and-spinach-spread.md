# Goat Cheese with Honey & Spinach Spread

## Details

Whenever I make any version of spinach dip, nothing in the pantry is spared. This is a more tempered cousin of that dip. The hot sauce is not optional. Make a batch up to a week ahead and chill it until a snack fit strikes.

## Ingredients

- 1 (10 oz) bag frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 1 (8 oz) block softened cream cheese
- 1 (4 oz) log softened goat cheese
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 Tbs olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 3 garlic cloves
- 2 tsp onion powder
- Zest and juice of 1 large lemon
- 2–3 tsp hot sauce or buffalo sauce
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 Cup shredded Parmesan or Pecorino

## Steps

1. Squeeze the spinach dry. Blend until smooth, then pulse in the spinach and Parmesan.

## Full

Squeeze the spinach to remove all the water: In a clean kitchen cloth or a few stacked sheets of paper towels, wrap the thawed spinach and squeeze it tightly over the sink to remove all excess water. Set aside.

Blend the spread/dip: In a food processor fitted with a steel blade, blend the cream cheese, goat cheese, honey, olive oil, mustard, garlic, onion powder, lemon zest and juice, hot sauce, salt, and pepper flakes on high until smooth. Remove the lid and add the spinach and Parmesan and pulse until just combined. Transfer the mixture to a lidded container and refrigerate until ready to use.

## Ingredients

- frozen spinach, 1 (10-ounce) bag, thawed
- plain cream cheese, 1 (8-ounce) block, softened
- plain goat cheese, 1 (4-ounce) log, softened
- honey, 2 teaspoons
- olive oil, 2 tablespoons, plus more for drizzling
- Dijon mustard, 1 teaspoon
- garlic cloves, 3
- onion powder, 2 teaspoons
- lemon, 1 large, zested and juiced
- hot sauce (such as RedHot) or Buffalo sauce, 2 to 3 teaspoons
- kosher salt, 1/2 teaspoon
- crushed red pepper flakes, 1/2 teaspoon
- shredded Parmesan cheese or Pecorino Romano, 1/2 cup (2 ounces), plus more for sprinkling

## Instructions

**Squeeze the spinach to remove all the water:** In a clean kitchen cloth or a few stacked sheets of paper towels, wrap the thawed spinach and squeeze it tightly over the sink to remove all excess water. Set aside.

**Blend the spread/dip:** In a food processor fitted with a steel blade, blend the cream cheese, goat cheese, honey, olive oil, mustard, garlic, onion powder, lemon zest and juice, hot sauce, salt, and pepper flakes on high until smooth. Remove the lid and add the spinach and Parmesan and pulse until just combined. Transfer the mixture to a lidded container and refrigerate until ready to use.
