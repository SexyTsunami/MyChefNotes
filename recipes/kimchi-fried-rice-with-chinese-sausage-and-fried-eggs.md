# Kimchi Fried Rice with Chinese Sausage and Fried Eggs

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cup kimchi
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 5 green onions, chopped
- olive oil
- 1 Chinese sausage link
- 4–6 cups cooked rice
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1–2 Tbsp gochujang red pepper paste
- seasoned soy bean paste
- 1/8–1/4 cup soy sauce
- 4–6 fried eggs, sunny side up
- more green onions to garnish

## Steps

1. Fry the kimchi, garlic, green onions, and sausage, then the rice with sesame oil and soy. Top with fried eggs.

## Full

1. Warm kimchi in cast iron skillet, med high heat.
2. Move kimchi to perimeter of pan and add olive oil to center to heat.
3. Add garlic and green onions until garlic lightly golden. Then sausage.
4. Add rice and drizzle sesame oil.
5. Add soy sauce to taste.
6. In separate pan, fry eggs in olive oil.
7. Serve rice with eggs and green onions on top.
