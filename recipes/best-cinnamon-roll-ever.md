# Best Cinnamon Roll Ever

## Details

Filling

## Ingredients

- Dough: 4 cups bread flour = 508 gm
- Dough: 1 3/4 cup whole milk
- Dough: 4 tsp dry active yeast
- Dough: 2 tsp salt
- Dough: 1/4 cup sugar = 50 gm
- Dough: 1 egg
- Dough: 1/4 cup butter
- Filling: 1/4 cup melted butter
- Filling: 3/4 cup golden brown sugar
- Filling: 2 Tbsp ground cinnamon
- Filling: 1/4 cup granulated sugar

## Optional

- Filling: fig / raisins

## Steps

1. Knead the dough 5–10 min and rise 3–4 hours. Fill, cut 12 rolls, rise 30 min–1 hour, and bake at 350°F for 30–40 min.

## Full

1. Whisk dry ingredients. Microwave milk until warm. Add egg and butter to milk and whisk until butter is melted.
2. Add half of dry mixture to all of the wet mixture to create a wet paste. Place into KitchenAid mixing bowl.
3. Put on dough hook attachment and set to speed 4. Slowly incorporate rest of dry mixture.
4. Continue to mix with dough hook until gluten forms (dough will be stringy and stick back to itself). Takes about 5–10 min.
5. Place in warm place covered with saran wrap. Let rise for 3–4 hours. (Optional: place in fridge overnight after the 3–4 hours.)
1. After rolling out dough a bit larger than a standard half sheet pan, using extra flour to prevent sticking, spread melted butter evenly on surface of dough.
2. Combine cinnamon and sugar and sprinkle evenly on melted butter. May add raisins.
3. Roll dough into a log and cut into 12 rolls. Place in a pan and let rise for 30 min–1 hour.
4. Heat oven to 350°F and bake for 30–40 min.
5. Add dollop of cream cheese frosting to each roll prior to serving. See Whipped Cream Cheese Frosting.
