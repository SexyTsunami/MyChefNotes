# Golabki (Polish) — Ground Beef + Cabbage Stew

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 1 head cabbage, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, chopped, minced
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 Tbsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup water

## Steps

1. Brown the beef, then simmer with cabbage, onion, garlic, and tomatoes until the cabbage is tender.

## Full

1. In Dutch oven, heat and brown beef until cooked. Drain. Stir in cabbage, onion, garlic, and water. Add tomatoes and seasoning. Add water. Bring to boil and simmer until cabbage is tender. May cover Dutch oven to speed up process.
